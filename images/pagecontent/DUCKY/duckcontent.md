# DUCKY
## Can we make robots less uncanny?

Humans are trying to make robots looks as human as possible, literaly, but is this what actually works? With my colleague Giulio Dalla Porta we tried to envision a more friendly robot aestethic.

The brief of the project was to develope a concept of a robotic element for the urban life of the future. We decided to focus on waste collection and specifically on the littering issue. We decided to go for a “companion” that can blend in the urban life and act as an inhabitant of the city itself. In the beginning we putted a lot of effort in understanding what was not working with already existing one. Robots like “Sofia” or “Spot” are very uncanny and tends to scare most of the people. In a way they feel wrong. Other designs instead, specifically the ones coming from toy design tend to be more familiar and joyful. After sketching and some physical model, we realized some first shapes of the robot. We used soft curves and colored parts to comunicate playfulness, like in toy design. To reduce the uncanny feeling we introduced a dynamic display that allow the companion “Ducky” to change espression based on the situation he is involved in: green  when a task is performed, red light if he needs to be helped or yellow for standby. We decided to insert colored orange parts to signal the ones that can moves and act in the real world: the mouth to collect waste, the legs and the tail for movement purposes. We conceptualized also an “home” for the duck, that is used to recharge the batteries and store the trash that is collected.
