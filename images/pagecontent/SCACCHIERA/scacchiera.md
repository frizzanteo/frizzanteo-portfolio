# SCACCHIERA
## One you can completely take apart

I'm always surprised about the amount of waste that art prodcution leave behind. I'm not saying that is wrong or right, but I wanted to explore the idea of an art piece that leave no waste. This is why I developed this chess set that can be completely disassembled.

I was deeply inspired by the work of Lorenzo Damiani. Ciotola 152, Lampada packlight, Fel3 and H20. Using what's already there to create something new. Finding the poetic in what is everyday in front of our eyes. In this case, screws and nuts for the pieces, and leftovers materials for the board.

It makes me feel good that what I'm making will not last forever.

The set counts 32 pieces, 16 for each side made with the same screws and nuts.
The black set is then treated to obtain a darker color and make the game playable. The treatment still preserve the possibility of taking all the nuts and bolts apart.

The board is made out of 2 sheets of polycarbonate that holds scrap pieces of wood together with 4 screws and nuts.

The "soul of the chessboard" is contained inbetween two layers of new materials, to symbolize the will of the new generation to take care of what was left behind.
